Where your data is saved

Firebase Firestore — daily data
Collection: foziya_days
Every checklist tick, rating, mood, and note saves here. Each day is one document. View at console.firebase.google.com → Firestore → foziya_days.

Firebase Storage — photos
Folder: foziya/photos/
Every photo saves to Firebase Storage. View at console.firebase.google.com → Storage → foziya folder. Photos named by date and time.

Firebase Firestore — weekly notes
Collection: foziya_weeks
Weekly reflections save here. Document ID is the year and week number, e.g. 2025_W24.
Install this app on your Android
📲 Method 1 — Install banner:
If you see the install banner at the top of this page, tap Install. Done.

📲 Method 2 — Chrome menu:
Tap the three-dot ⋮ menu at the top right of Chrome → tap "Add to Home screen" → tap Add.

The app icon appears on your home screen. Tap it to open without browser — works offline too.
Firebase free limits
Firebase free tier (Spark plan) gives you:
• 1 GB Storage for photos
• 50,000 Firestore reads per day
• 20,000 Firestore writes per day
• 10 GB bandwidth per month

This is far more than enough for personal daily skincare tracking. You will never need to pay.
